 LUNAR LEGACY — Interactive Off-World Heritage Atlas

> **Mapping, cataloging, and protecting humanity's historic spacecraft, rovers, and landing sites across the Moon and Mars.**  
> *Built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge*

**Live Interactive App:** [https://xlmo66.github.io/lunar-legacy/](https://xlmo66.github.io/lunar-legacy/)

---

 Overview

Since 1959, humanity has left behind **104+ documented sites** and over **200 metric tons of historic hardware** across the Moon and Mars—from *Luna 2* and *Apollo 11's Tranquility Base* to *Curiosity* and the *Ingenuity Mars Helicopter*. 

As lunar and Martian exploration accelerates under the **Artemis program** and commercial lander missions, these irreplaceable milestones of human achievement face growing risks from rocket plume blast ejecta, dust abrasion, and rover traffic. **LUNAR LEGACY** transforms fragmented planetary coordinates and orbital imagery into an accessible, interactive mission-control atlas for researchers, students, and mission planners.

---

 Key Features

- **Dual-World Interactive Cartography:** Switch seamlessly between high-resolution **Moon** and **Mars** coordinate maps with clickable mission pins and regional sector overlays.
- **Chronological Era Filtering:** Explore off-world history across distinct eras—from the *Early Space Race (1959–1968)* and *Apollo Era (1969–1972)* to *Modern Mars Rovers* and *Artemis-Era Landers*.
- **Telemetry & Heritage Dossiers:** Inspect exact planetary coordinates (`lat/lon`), landing dates, mission status, hardware mass, historical significance, and orbital verification notes for each site.
- **Artemis Heritage Protection Zones:** Visualizes recommended safety buffer radii (`500 m – 2 km`) aligned with NASA's *Recommendations to Space-Faring Entities* to protect historic sites from landing plume damage.
- **Zero-Backend Portability:** Engineered as a lightweight, self-contained single-page web application (`index.html`) that runs instantly in any modern browser with zero installation or server dependencies.

---

 NASA Open Data & Sources

LUNAR LEGACY synthesizes coordinates, imagery, and mission telemetry from open planetary archives:
- **NASA Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO) / LROC QuickMap** — High-resolution orbital verification of Apollo, Surveyor, and Luna landing sites.
- **NASA Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter (MRO) / HiRISE** — Orbital imagery and coordinates of Mars landers, rovers, and backshells.
- **NASA Planetary Data System (PDS) & NSSDC Master Catalog** — Historical mission logs, launch/landing dates, and spacecraft specifications.
- **NASA Artemis Accords & Heritage Guidelines** — Exclusion zone parameters for lunar heritage preservation.
